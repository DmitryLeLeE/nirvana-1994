FAQ

the questions people ask — answered without a museum tone

Q: Why is the band called Nirvana?
A: Kurt picked it. He wanted something pretty, not an aggressive punk word. The dictionary says: freedom from pain, suffering and the external world. He said that was pretty close to his idea of punk rock.

Q: What is grunge, really?
A: A label that stuck to the Seattle sound of the late 80s — heavy, slow, built from punk and metal, played by people in thrift-store flannel. Half a joke at first, then a fashion, then a museum exhibit. The whole story is here.

Q: Was Kurt married?
A: Yes. He married Courtney Love of the band Hole on February 24, 1992, on a beach in Hawaii. He wore pajamas.

Q: Who is Frances Bean Cobain?
A: His daughter, born August 18, 1992. She grew up away from the spotlight and became a visual artist. More here.

Q: Did Kurt write all the songs?
A: Almost all of them — music and words. "Marigold," the B-side of "Heart-Shaped Box," is Dave Grohl's.

Q: Why did Nirvana end?
A: Because Kurt died on April 5, 1994. There was no version of the band after that. The other two never replaced him.

Q: Is the "Kurt is still alive" thing true?
A: No. It is one of those stories people tell because the truth is too plain. He died in 1994, and the case was investigated and closed. The music is what's alive.

Q: What does "Boddah" mean?
A: Boddah was Kurt's imaginary friend from childhood. His last letter was addressed to him.

Q: What guitars did Kurt play?
A: Cheap left-handed pawnshop guitars, mostly: Fender Mustangs and Jaguars, a Univox Hi-Flier early on. The full list, with pictures, is here.

Q: What is the 27 Club?
A: The sad coincidence of musicians who died at 27 — Hendrix, Joplin, Morrison, Kurt. We wrote a whole page about why it isn't a club.

Q: Where can I hear the music?
A: The Media page has Spotify, the official YouTube channel, and three full videos embedded right there.

Q: Why does this site look like 1994?
A: Because that's when it happened, and because the web I grew up on looked like this. I keep it that way on purpose.

Q: Can I copy the lyrics from this page?
A: They're here to read and sing along. The songs belong to the band and the people who hold the rights — this page just types them in by hand, like we all did.
