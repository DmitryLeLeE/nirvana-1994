the whole story, year by year

YearWhat happened
1967Kurt Donald Cobain born in Aberdeen, Washington, February 20
1976Kurt's parents divorce; he is nine
1984drops out of high school; forms his first band Fecal Matter
1985meets Krist Novoselic through the Melvins
1987Nirvana forms; first demos recorded
1988debut single "Love Buzz" on Sub Pop Singles Club
1989Bleach released in June on Sub Pop; first US tour
1990Dave Grohl joins on drums; "Sliver" single
1991Nevermind released September 24 on DGC; "Smells Like Teen Spirit" explodes
1992marries Courtney Love (Feb 24); daughter Frances born (Aug 18); headlining Reading Festival; Incesticide
1993In Utero released September 21; MTV Unplugged recorded November 18
1994overdose in Rome (Mar 4); leaves rehab (Apr 1); dies in Seattle (Apr 5); public vigil (Apr 10)
