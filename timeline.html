Timeline

the whole story, year by year

YearWhat happened
1967Kurt Donald Cobain born in Aberdeen, Washington, February 20
1976Kurt's parents divorce; he is nine
1984drops out of high school; forms his first band Fecal Matter
1985meets Krist Novoselic through the Melvins
1987Nirvana forms; first demos recorded
1988debut single "Love Buzz" on Sub Pop Singles Club
1989Bleach released in June on Sub Pop; first US tour
1990Dave Grohl joins on drums; "Sliver" single
1991Nevermind released September 24 on DGC; "Smells Like Teen Spirit" enters MTV Buzz Bin; first Late Night with Arsenio Hall performance
Jan 1992Nevermind hits #1 on Billboard 200, knocking Michael Jackson's Dangerous from the top — nobody saw this coming, including the band
Feb 1992Kurt marries Courtney Love on Waikiki Beach, Hawaii — he wears pajamas
Aug 1992Frances Bean Cobain born; MTV VMAs — Kurt teases "Rape Me" opening chords before switching to "Lithium"; Nirvana wins Best Alternative Video
Aug 1992Reading Festival headline — Kurt arrives in a wheelchair, wearing a hospital gown; the set is considered one of the greatest live performances in rock history
Dec 1992Incesticide rarities compilation; Kurt writes liner notes attacking Spin magazine readers who missed the point of his songs
Feb 1993In Utero recorded in 14 days at Pachyderm Studio, Minnesota, with Steve Albini; DGC unhappy with the result; Scott Litt called in to remix two tracks
Sept 1993In Utero released; Walmart refuses to stock it; "Radio Friendly Unit Shifter" is not radio friendly
Nov 1993MTV Unplugged taped at Sony Music Studios, New York, November 18; set decorated with lilies and candles; last public performance Nirvana ever gives
Mar 1994Kurt overdoses on champagne and Rohypnol in Rome, March 4; Courtney tells press it was an accident; the band's European tour cancelled
Mar 1994Kurt returns to Seattle; Courtney organizes an intervention; March 25, he agrees to enter Exodus Recovery Center in Los Angeles
Apr 1994April 1: Kurt escapes from Exodus, flies back to Seattle on a commercial flight; nobody sees him for four days
Apr 5, 1994Kurt Donald Cobain dies in Seattle; he is 27 years old; his body is found on April 8 by an electrician
Apr 10, 1994public vigil at Seattle Center, 7,000 people; Courtney reads excerpts from the suicide note over PA; Kurt's ashes are scattered at the Wishkah River
