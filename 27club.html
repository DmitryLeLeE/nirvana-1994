The 27 Club

"The 27 Club" is the name people give to a sad coincidence: a list of famous musicians who died at the age of twenty-seven. Kurt Cobain is on that list, and he never wanted to be. Nobody asks to join.

MusicianBandLived
Robert Johnsonblues1911–1938
Brian JonesThe Rolling Stones1942–1969
Jimi HendrixThe Jimi Hendrix Experience1942–1970
Janis JoplinBig Brother & the Holding Company1943–1970
Jim MorrisonThe Doors1943–1971
Kurt CobainNirvana1967–1994
Amy Winehousesolo1983–2011

The number itself is not magic. It is a coincidence that got a name, and the name started to sound like a club with a door. There is no door. There is just a list of people who happened to die young, and a lot of music that stopped too early.

What matters about Kurt is not the age he died at. It is the songs, and the way they still sound like somebody telling the truth. That is the part worth keeping.
