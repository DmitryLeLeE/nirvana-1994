this is not me. this is the best self-portrait i can draw in text.

I'm just a fan. I don't have a radio show, I don't write for a magazine, I never met the band. I heard the songs, and they stayed.

I made this page by hand because the web I grew up on looked like this — tables and marquees and guestbooks — and because a museum-style site about Kurt would be wrong. Kurt never sounded like a museum.

Everything here is typed by hand: the pages, the lyrics, the dates. If something is wrong, tell me in the guestbook. If something here matters to you, tell me that too.

I keep this page the way some people keep a candle: it doesn't fix anything, it just stays lit. Some newer facts (like his 2002 Journals or later archival releases) were added later to keep the record complete, even though the site still lives in 1994.
