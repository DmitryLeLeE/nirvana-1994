Books

  • Charles R. Cross — Heavier Than Heaven: A Biography of Kurt Cobain (2001) — the big biography
  • Michael Azerrad — Come As You Are: The Story of Nirvana (1993) — the only book written with the band while it existed
  • Kurt Cobain — Journals (2002) — his notebooks, published after his death
  • Danny Goldberg — Serving the Servant: Remembering Kurt Cobain (2019) — by their former manager
  • Cobain on Cobain (2016) — collected interviews, 1987–1994

Films

  • Kurt Cobain: Montage of Heck — Brett Morgen (2015) — the documentary built from his own tapes and notebooks
  • Kurt Cobain: About a Son — AJ Schnack (2006) — Kurt talking, over images of the places he lived
  • Nirvana: Live! Tonight! Sold Out!! (1994) — the band's own concert film
  • 1991: The Year Punk Broke — Dave Markey (1992) — the tour with Sonic Youth
