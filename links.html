Disputed details (such as the exact date of Nirvana’s first 1987 house-party gig versus the Raymond, WA recording) are marked explicitly. Any illustrative reconstructions (such as simulated 1994 Usenet threads or zine covers) are clearly labeled.

3. Essential Books

  • Michael Azerrad — Come As You Are: The Story of Nirvana (1993). Written with the band’s direct participation before 1994.
  • Charles R. Cross — Heavier Than Heaven: A Biography of Kurt Cobain (2001). Standard biography (note: some early 1987 show dates were later revised by LiveNirvana).
  • Kurt Cobain — Journals (Riverhead Books, 2002). Facsimile notebooks, drawings, Top 50 Albums list.
  • Gillian G. Gaar — In Utero (33⅓, 2006) & Entertain Us: The Rise of Nirvana (2012). Detailed studio and Sub Pop history.
  • Everett True — Nirvana: The Biography (2006). Firsthand Seattle/UK press witness account.
  • Danny Goldberg — Serving the Servant: Remembering Kurt Cobain (2019). Account by Nirvana’s former co-manager.
  • Nick Soulsby — Cobain on Cobain: Interviews and Encounters (2016). Collected 1987–1994 interviews.

4. Documentary Films & Periodicals (1990s)

  • 1991: The Year Punk Broke (Dave Markey, 1992) — European tour with Sonic Youth.
  • Nirvana: Live! Tonight! Sold Out!! (1994) — band-assembled concert/TV compilation.
  • Kurt Cobain: About a Son (AJ Schnack, 2006) — Azerrad interview tapes over Aberdeen/Olympia/Seattle footage.
  • Kurt Cobain: Montage of Heck (Brett Morgen, 2015) — home tapes, art, and notebooks.
  • Periodicals: Rolling Stone (Apr 1992; Jan 1994; Jun 1994), Sassy (Apr 1992), Los Angeles Times (Jun 1994), Rockerilla (Sep 1989).
