I built this site by hand, one page at a time, because that is how we did it back then. No editors, no templates. Just Notepad, a modem, and too much coffee.

If you are reading this, you are the kind of person who clicks on things and reads the source. Stay that way. The internet was better when people looked under the hood.

Kurt once said punk is musical freedom. This little page is my freedom. That's all this whole site is, really — a thank-you note that got out of hand.
