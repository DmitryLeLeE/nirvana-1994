The numbers

Nevermind has sold more than 30 million copies worldwide. On January 11, 1992, it reached number one on the Billboard 200 — pushing Michael Jackson's Dangerous out of the top spot. "Smells Like Teen Spirit" is one of the most-played rock songs ever written, and its video was the first grunge video to pass a billion views on YouTube.
