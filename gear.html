Guitars and Sound

how the noise was made

The guitars

Kurt was left-handed, and left-handed guitars were rare, so he played whatever cheap lefty he could find in pawnshops. The ones he kept coming back to:

GuitarWhen
Univox Hi-Flierthe early days, through the Bleach years
Fender Mustanghis main guitar from 1991 on — he bought several
Fender Jaguarused a lot in 1993–1994, the In Utero tours
Mosriteone of the ones hanging in the museum — he used it around the Nevermind era
Martin acousticthe Unplugged set

he also painted on his guitars and smashed the cheap ones at the end of shows


the Teen Spirit guitar, a Mosrite, and a smashed one — Museum of Pop Culture, Seattle

The pedals

The distortion was a Boss DS-1, and from 1992 a Boss DS-2. The watery guitar on "Come As You Are" is an Electro-Harmonix Small Clone chorus — one pedal, one setting, and the riff does the rest.

How the albums were recorded

AlbumHow it went down
Bleachabout three days at Reciprocal Recording in Seattle, for roughly six hundred dollars, with Jack Endino
Nevermindwith Butch Vig at Sound City in Los Angeles, spring 1991 — polished, layered, loud
In Uterowith Steve Albini at Pachyderm Studio in Minnesota, February 1993 — two weeks, recorded almost live, no polish on purpose
Unpluggedone night in New York, November 18, 1993, with candles and lilies on stage — Kurt asked for it to look like a funeral
