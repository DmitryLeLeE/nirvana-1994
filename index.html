Nirvana was three people from Aberdeen, Washington, who changed rock music forever. Kurt Cobain wrote the songs, sang them, and carried a weight he could not put down. This page is about all of it — the band, the songs, his daughter Frances, and what happened on April 5, 1994.

Some bands you just listen to. Nirvana you carry with you.

Kurt wrote about the things people feel but don't say — the ache, the loneliness, the anger — and made them sound beautiful instead of shameful. That's why I made this page. It isn't a museum piece. It's a thank-you note, kept the only way I know: a little corner of the internet, the way it looked back then.
