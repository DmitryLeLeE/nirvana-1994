NIRVANA

in memory of Kurt Cobain

February 20, 1967 — April 5, 1994

Home | The Band | Discography | Kurt Cobain | Death & Legacy | Songs | Media | Guestbook | Sources

Nirvana was a band from Aberdeen, Washington, that changed rock music in the early 1990s. Kurt Cobain, Krist Novoselic and Dave Grohl made three studio albums — and one of them, Nevermind, changed everything.

This page is about the band, their songs, Kurt himself, and his daughter Frances Bean Cobain. It is also about what happened on April 5, 1994, and the music that outlived him.
