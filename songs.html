Songs

the essential tracks, in our own words — no lyrics reprinted

Smells Like Teen Spirit · 1991

The song that started everything. The title came from a joke: a friend sprayed "Kurt Smells Like Teen Spirit" on his wall, Teen Spirit being a deodorant. A joke turned into the anthem of a generation.

Come As You Are · 1991

Slow, with a guitar riff you can't forget. "Come as you are" — acceptance, no conditions.

Lithium · 1991

Named after the drug prescribed for bipolar disorder. A song about mood swings — one line "I'm so happy," the next "I'm so lonely."

In Bloom · 1991

About fans who sang along without understanding what the songs were about. It frustrated Kurt to be heard as background music.

About a Girl · 1989

An early song from Bleach, written after a breakup. Kurt worried it sounded too pop, but it stayed.

Sliver · 1990

A loud, simple song about being left at his grandparents' house as a child. Raw and almost funny on the surface, lonely underneath.

Aneurysm · 1992

From Incesticide. A fierce live favorite, written for the band's early drummer era; people argue about the exact meaning, which is part of its pull.

Heart-Shaped Box · 1993

From In Utero, widely read as being about Courtney Love, though Kurt gave vague answers. Dark, heavy, tense in the chorus.

Rape Me · 1993

Against violence — and against the way the press and the industry invaded his life. The title is deliberately brutal so it can't be ignored.

Dumb · 1993

About feeling "not right" and numbing yourself. Quiet, almost lullaby-like in shape, and sad underneath.

Pennyroyal Tea · 1993

A dark joke about stomach pain: pennyroyal is an herb with a grim history, and Kurt sang about how physically bad he felt.

All Apologies · 1993

The closing song of In Utero. Kurt called it a song for his wife and daughter: "everything I have to say is sorry." Many hear it as a goodbye.

Where Did You Sleep Last Night · 1994

A folk song from Lead Belly, recorded on MTV Unplugged. The last track on the album — and one of the most haunting recordings Kurt left behind.
