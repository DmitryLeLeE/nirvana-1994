Family

Courtney Love

Courtney Love — born July 9, 1964 — was the singer and guitarist of the band Hole, and a force in her own right before she ever met Kurt. They met in 1990 at a club in Portland. The two of them were loud, difficult, and in love.

They married on February 24, 1992, on a beach in Hawaii, with Kurt wearing pajamas. Their daughter Frances Bean was born a few months later, on August 18, 1992.

The marriage was intense and, at the end, troubled — two people fighting addiction and fame at the same time. After Kurt's death, Courtney continued with Hole and later with acting and solo music. Whatever people argue about her, she is the person Kurt chose, and the mother of his child.

Frances Bean Cobain

Frances Bean Cobain was born August 18, 1992. She was not yet two when her father died, so she never really knew him — she knows him through the songs, the films, and other people's memories.

She was raised mostly by Kurt's side of the family and grew into a visual artist. As an adult she has spoken quietly about what it is like to carry his name: she does not want to be a symbol, just a person. She has said she loves him, and wishes she could have known him longer.
