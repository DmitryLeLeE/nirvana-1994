ASCII Guitar Tabs

transcribed by ear, saved as .txt — originally traded on alt.music.nirvana & OLGA

In 1994 there are no video lessons. You sit next to your cassette deck, hit rewind until the tape stretches, and type out six lines of dashes in Notepad.

Tip from a pawnshop Fender player: turn your Boss DS-1 tone knob around 11 o’clock, distortion all the way up, and don’t worry if your strings buzz. Kurt’s did too.
